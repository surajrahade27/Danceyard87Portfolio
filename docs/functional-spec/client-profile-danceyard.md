## 9. Notes Before Publishing

- **Brochure photos:** the dancer and runway photos may be stock or third-party images. Confirm the client owns them or has permission before using them on the site.
- **Lakmé Fashion Week:** the brochure shows the Lakmé Fashion Week logo. Use the name in text only, and don't use the logo unless the client has permission.
- **Choreographer names:** Ganesh Acharya, Saroj Khan and Ganesh Hegde are named as people the mentors have worked with, not as Dance Yard staff. Keep that wording.
- **Photo and video shoots:** the Instagram bio lists these as a service, but the brochure doesn't mention them. Ask what the service is (shoots for students' portfolios and reels?) and whether it gets its own section on the site.
- **"Mumbai-based":** the academy is in Pune. The brochure explains that the mentors travel from Mumbai, so on the site pair "Mumbai-based" with that explanation rather than using it on its own.
- **Numbers:** confirm 20+ years, 35+ dance forms and 100+ dancers placed in Bollywood are current.
